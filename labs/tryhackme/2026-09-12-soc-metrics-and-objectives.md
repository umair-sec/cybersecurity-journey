# SOC Metrics and Objectives

## Overview / Objective
Understand how a Security Operations Center measures and improves its performance.

## What I did / key steps
Learned the key metrics:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **AC**: Alert Count | Overall SOC alert workload |
| **FPR**: False Positive Rate | Excessive alert noise and alert fatigue |
| **AER**: Alert Escalation Rate | How alerts move from L1 to L2 |
| **TDR**: Threat Detection Rate | How effectively real threats are detected |
| **MTTD** | Mean Time to Detect |
| **MTTA** | Mean Time to Acknowledge |
| **MTTR** | Mean Time to Respond |
| **SLA** | Expected detection, acknowledgement and response times |

In the practical scenarios, I worked out **which metric was causing a problem** and chose the right improvement: detection rule tuning, real-time alerting, better workload distribution, faster escalation, or documented incident-response workbooks.

## Findings / outcome
Room completed, including the scenario-based exercises.

## Lessons learned
Generating an alert is only the start. An effective SOC has to detect, investigate, escalate and respond before the attacker reaches their goal.
