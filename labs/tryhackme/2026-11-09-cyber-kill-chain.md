# Cyber Kill Chain

## Overview / Objective
Map attack techniques to Cyber Kill Chain stages (practical based on the 2013 Target data breach). Concepts were previously studied on LetsDefend.

## Platform & date
| Platform | Room | Completed |
|---|---|---|
| TryHackMe | Cyber Kill Chain | 9 November 2026 |

## What I did / key steps
1. Reconnaissance and Weaponization
2. Delivery and Exploitation
3. Installation and Persistence
4. Command and Control (C2)
5. Actions on Objectives

## Key concepts
Cyber Kill Chain, LetsDefend, MITRE

## Lessons learned
Understanding the stages of an attack helps a SOC analyst connect individual security events to the bigger picture of an intrusion, and distinguish initial access, persistence, C2 communication and final objectives.

**Next step:** Continue building Blue Team and SOC investigation skills, one lab at a time.
