# SOC Role in Blue Team

## Overview / Objective
Understand the different roles within a SOC and the wider blue team.

## Platform & date
| Platform | Module | Completed |
|---|---|---|
| TryHackMe | SOC Role in Blue Team | 27 August 2026 |

## What I did / key steps
- Compared L1 and L2 analysts in real SOCs
- Covered CIRT/CERT teams
- Compared an internal SOC with an MSSP
- Completed the **"Who to Call"** scenario, matching incidents to the right team

## Findings / outcome
The difference between L1 and L2 is not only experience, but also how deeply you investigate an alert before escalating it.

## Lessons learned
Routing an incident to the right team sounds obvious, until you're under pressure and everything looks urgent.
