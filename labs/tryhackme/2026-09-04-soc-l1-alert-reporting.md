# SOC L1 Alert Reporting

## Overview / Objective
Learn how to document alerts properly and when to escalate to L2.

## Platform & date
| Platform | Module | Completed |
|---|---|---|
| TryHackMe | SOC L1 Alert Reporting | 4 September 2026 |

## What I did / key steps
- Wrote alert reports using the **5 Ws**: Who, What, When, Where, Why
- Learned the criteria for escalating to L2

## Key concepts
- The 5 Ws structure for alert reports
- Escalation decisions: not every alert needs escalating, and knowing which ones do is harder than it looks

## Findings / outcome
- The 5 Ws sound obvious until you try to write a report under time pressure and realise you missed half the details.
- Senior analysts really do read these reports. Good documentation saves them from starting from zero, and bad documentation wastes everyone's time.

## Lessons learned
Good reporting is easy to forget when you're just trying to close alerts quickly. I'm still improving at it, but now I know what good looks like.
