# Security Principles

## Overview / Objective
Strengthen my understanding of the core concepts behind cybersecurity.

## Platform & date
| Platform | Room | Completed |
|---|---|---|
| TryHackMe | Security Principles | 17 September 2026 |

## What I did / key steps
- **CIA Triad** (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) and its opposite, the **DAD Triad** (Disclosure, Alteration, Destruction/Denial)
- Security models: Bell-LaPadula, Biba and Clark-Wilson
- Authenticity and non-repudiation
- Defence in Depth
- Trust but Verify and Zero Trust
- Microsegmentation
- Vulnerability, threat and risk
- Shared responsibility in cloud security

## Key concepts
CIA vs DAD, formal security models, Zero Trust and microsegmentation, and the vulnerability/threat/risk relationship.

## Findings / outcome
Room completed. This builds the foundation for Security+ Domain 1 and for SOC work.

## Lessons learned
Good security is not about making one control as strong as possible. It is about understanding the risks, applying the right controls, and keeping the right balance between confidentiality, integrity and availability.
