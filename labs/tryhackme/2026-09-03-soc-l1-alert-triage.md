# SOC L1 Alert Triage

## Overview / Objective
Understand how Tier 1 analysts handle, prioritise and investigate security alerts in a SIEM dashboard.

## Platform & date
| Platform | Module | Completed |
|---|---|---|
| TryHackMe | SOC L1 Alert Triage | 3 September 2026 |

## What I did / key steps
1. Broke down alert properties: severity, status, verdict, and technical indicators (source IPs, usernames, URLs).
2. Applied prioritisation logic: filtering out noise, sorting by severity and checking timestamps so nothing slips through.
3. Walked through the full triage lifecycle:
   - taking ownership of the alert
   - moving it to *In Progress*
   - analysing the logs
   - deciding **True Positive vs False Positive**
   - closing the alert

## Key concepts
Alert properties, prioritisation and the triage lifecycle.

## Findings / outcome
Completed the hands-on lab.

## Lessons learned
Having a consistent triage process makes sure that high-severity alerts are handled first and nothing is missed.
