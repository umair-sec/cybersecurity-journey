# SOC Workbooks & Lookups

## Overview / Objective
Learn how L1 analysts investigate alerts in a structured way, using workbooks and context sources.

## Platform & date
| Platform | Section | Completed |
|---|---|---|
| TryHackMe | SOC Workbooks & Lookups | 9 September 2026 |

## What I did / key steps
- Used SOC workbooks/playbooks for step-by-step triage
- Enriched alerts with identity, asset and threat-intel information
- Correlated everything with SIEM logs
- Learned when to escalate
- Relied on identity and asset inventories
- Checked network diagrams for context
- Used lookups to add useful information during investigations

## Key concepts
Enrichment sources: identity inventory, asset inventory, threat intel, network diagrams and lookups.

## Findings / outcome
Section completed.

## Lessons learned
An alert by itself almost never tells the full story. Context is what turns an alert into a proper investigation.
